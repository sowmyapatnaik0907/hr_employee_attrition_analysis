# 1. PROJECT INTRODUCTION

Employee attrition is one of the major challenges faced by organizations. Employee attrition refers to employees leaving an organization voluntarily or involuntarily over a particular period.

When employees leave an organization, companies may face several challenges, including recruitment expenses, training costs, reduced productivity, workload distribution problems, and loss of experienced employees.

Understanding why employees leave is important for Human Resources (HR) departments. By analyzing employee-related data, organizations can identify patterns and employee groups that have higher attrition rates.

This project, **HR Employee Attrition Analysis**, focuses on analyzing employee data using Python and SQL. The project identifies patterns associated with employee attrition and presents the findings through an interactive dashboard.

The analysis focuses on factors such as:

- Overtime
- Job satisfaction
- Department
- Job role
- Monthly income
- Employee age
- Years at company
- Business travel
- Work-life balance
- Marital status

The final objective is to provide meaningful insights and recommendations that can support better HR decision-making.

# 2. PROBLEM STATEMENT

Organizations often experience employee attrition without having a clear understanding of the factors associated with employees leaving their jobs.

Simply knowing the total number of employees who left does not provide enough information for HR departments to make effective decisions.

The main problem addressed in this project is:

**"How can employee data be analyzed to identify patterns associated with employee attrition and help HR departments understand which employee groups may require attention?"**

This project uses Python and SQL to analyze employee records, calculate attrition rates, compare different employee categories, and identify potential areas of concern.

The results are presented through an interactive dashboard so that users can easily understand the analysis.

# 3. PROJECT OBJECTIVES

The main objectives of this project are:

1. To understand employee attrition using a structured employee dataset.
2. To calculate the total number of employees and employees who left the organization.
3. To calculate the overall employee attrition rate.
4. To analyze attrition across different departments.
5. To identify job roles with higher attrition rates.
6. To compare attrition between employees working overtime and those not working overtime.
7. To analyze the relationship between job satisfaction and attrition patterns.
8. To examine employee tenure and attrition.
9. To perform data analysis using Python
